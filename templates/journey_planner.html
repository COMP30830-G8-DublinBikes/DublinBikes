How to use it

This tool is designed to make route planning quick and practical.

1. Choose a station Pick a station with a suitable number of available bikes.
2. Add your destination Enter any place or address you want to cycle to.
3. Open directions Launch Google Maps with bicycling mode already selected.

Planning note

If you use your current location, the route starts from where you are now. Otherwise, it starts from the selected station location.
