How it helps

The interface is designed around the main decisions a rider makes before and during a trip.

01

Find a nearby station

Check live bike and dock numbers to quickly spot a practical station near your location.

02

Judge the conditions

Use current weather and short-term forecast context to decide whether cycling makes sense right now.

03

Continue the journey

Move straight into planning and directions without repeating the same search on another service.
